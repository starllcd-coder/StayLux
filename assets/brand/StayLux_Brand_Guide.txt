
StayLux Brand Guide
===================

Brand Name:
StayLux

Tagline:
Smart Management for Hotels & Residences

--------------------------------------------------
COLOR PALETTE
--------------------------------------------------
Primary Color (Navy): #0A192F
Secondary Color (Gold): #C9A24D
Accent Color (White): #FFFFFF
UI Gray (Optional): #6B7280

--------------------------------------------------
TYPOGRAPHY (OFFICIAL)
--------------------------------------------------

ENGLISH FONTS
-------------
Primary (Headings):
- Playfair Display
  or
- Libre Baskerville

Secondary (Body / UI):
- Inter (Recommended)
  or
- Poppins

--------------------------------------------------
ARABIC FONTS (GCC-READY)
--------------------------------------------------

Primary Arabic Font (Recommended):
- Cairo
  Usage:
  - Arabic UI
  - Forms & tables
  - Reports
  - Mobile apps

Secondary Arabic Font (Headings / Elegant):
- Tajawal
  Usage:
  - Page titles
  - Section headings
  - Marketing material

Alternative Corporate Arabic Font:
- IBM Plex Sans Arabic
  Usage:
  - Enterprise dashboards
  - Reports
  - Long text readability

--------------------------------------------------
FONT USAGE RULES
--------------------------------------------------
- English UI: Inter / Poppins
- Arabic UI: Cairo
- Arabic headings: Tajawal (optional)
- Maintain consistent numeral formatting with currency
- Ensure proper RTL alignment and spacing

--------------------------------------------------
DOCUMENTS & REPORTS
--------------------------------------------------
Invoices, contracts, receipts:
- English: Inter
- Arabic: Cairo

Bilingual documents:
- Arabic on right side
- English on left side
- Equal visual weight for both languages

--------------------------------------------------
MOBILE APPS
--------------------------------------------------
Android:
- Inter (English)
- Cairo (Arabic)

iOS:
- Inter (English)
- Cairo (Arabic)
(Font files must be bundled with the app)

--------------------------------------------------
FINAL RECOMMENDATION
--------------------------------------------------
If only one safe combination is used across all platforms:

English Font: Inter
Arabic Font: Cairo

This combination is modern, premium, readable, and widely accepted in GCC markets.
